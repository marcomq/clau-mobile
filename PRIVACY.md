# Privacy Policy

Clau (Android package `io.github.marcomq.clau`), by Marco Mengelkoch. Effective 2026-10-17.

Clau does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Microphone:** Clau uses the microphone, like the original Glo, to feed live sound into the instrument's
  effects. Audio is processed in memory on your device in real time. It is never recorded, saved or transmitted.
  You can deny the permission; Clau then runs without input.
- **Local settings:** your choice of microphone, mic sensitivity and the instrument's own settings (for example
  unlocked channels) are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall
  the app.
- **No network, no tracking:** Clau has no internet access, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party
  SDKs that collect data.

The full source code is public: https://github.com/marcomq/clau-mobile

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/marcomq/clau-mobile/issues

Changes to this policy are published in this file.
