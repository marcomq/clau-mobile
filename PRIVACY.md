# Privacy Policy

Flono (Android package `io.github.marcomq.flono`), by Marco Mengelkoch. Effective 2026-10-17.

Flono does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Microphone:** Flono uses the microphone, like the original Glo, to feed live sound into the instrument's
  effects. Audio is processed in memory on your device in real time. It is never recorded, saved or transmitted.
  You can deny the permission; Flono then runs without input.
- **Local settings:** your choice of microphone, mic sensitivity and the instrument's own settings (for example
  unlocked channels) are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall
  the app.
- **No network, no tracking:** Flono has no internet access, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party
  SDKs that collect data.

The full source code is public: https://github.com/marcomq/flono

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/marcomq/flono/issues

Changes to this policy are published in this file.
